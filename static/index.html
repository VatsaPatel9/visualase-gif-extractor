How it works

  1. Add GIFs on the left. Each file is read frame by frame (time, laser power, the numbered points and their temperatures).
  2. Check the fiber. The laser fiber (the dark "stick") is found on the anatomy image. Drag its ends if needed — the center between skull entry and tip is the reference for distances.
  3. Choose your output: which data, which points, which frames, Excel or CSV — then Run and download.
  4. Visualize the GIF with the fiber, points and distances drawn on top, plus a temperature chart; export it as an annotated GIF.

Drag the skull-entry and tip handles onto the dark fiber line (or press Place by clicking, then click the entry and then the tip). Snap to line fits the line precisely. The center is what distances are measured from. Fibers you save are remembered for this GIF.
